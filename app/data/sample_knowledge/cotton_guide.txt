Title: Cotton Fertilizer and Pest Management Best Practices
Category: Soil Nutrition & Pest Management
Crop: Cotton
Language: English
Source: Central Institute for Cotton Research (ICAR-CICR)

1. Fertilizer Schedule for Cotton:
- Balanced NPK: Recommended dose is 120:60:60 kg/ha for Bt hybrid cotton.
- Basal: 20% Nitrogen, 100% Phosphorus, 100% Potassium applied at sowing.
- Top Dressing: 40% Nitrogen at square formation (45 DAS) and remaining 40% at peak flowering/boll development (65-70 DAS).
- Micronutrients: Apply Zinc Sulphate (25 kg/ha) and Borax (10 kg/ha) to prevent boll shedding and square drop.
- Foliar Spray: Spray 2% DAP or 1% 19:19:19 with 1% Magnesium Sulphate at flowering to reduce leaf reddening and boll drop.

2. Precautions:
- Do not apply high nitrogen during early vegetative stages to prevent excessive vegetative height.
- Soil moisture must be adequate when top-dressing nitrogen fertilizers.
